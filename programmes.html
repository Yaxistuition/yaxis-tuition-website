Academic Programmes

Structured support from Primary to Higher Secondary, with Classes 10–12 exclusively for girl students.

Learning Programmes for Every Stage of Education.

Classes 1–5

Primary Education

Building strong academic foundations through reading, writing, basic mathematics, language development, and essential subject knowledge.

Classes 6–8

Middle School Education

Strengthening conceptual understanding, developing independent learning habits, and improving knowledge across all major subjects.

Classes 9–10

Secondary Education

Focused academic guidance, subject-wise learning support, regular practice, revision, and examination preparation.

Classes 11–12

Higher Secondary Education

Specialised academic support exclusively for girl students, focusing on subject understanding, individual learning difficulties, conceptual clarity, and examination preparation.
